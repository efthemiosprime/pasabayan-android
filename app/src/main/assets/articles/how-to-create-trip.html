How to Create a Trip

As a carrier on Pasabayan, creating a trip lets shippers know your travel route and availability. Once your trip is published, shippers with packages along your route can request a match. Follow the steps below to create your first trip.

Getting Started

Step 1: Open the Dashboard

Open the Pasabayan app and navigate to your Dashboard. In the Carrier section, tap the "Create Trip" button.

Step 2: Understand the Form

The trip creation form is organized into expandable sections. A progress banner at the top tracks 4 requirements that must be completed before you can submit:

  • Route — Pickup and dropoff locations
  • Weight — Available carrying capacity
  • Price — Your delivery pricing
  • Dates — Departure and arrival schedule

Tap any requirement chip to jump directly to that section. All 4 must be filled to enable the "Create Trip" button.

Section 1: Route Details

Set Your Pickup Location
  • Country: Select from the dropdown — Canada, United States, Philippines, or India
  • City: Start typing your departure city. Autocomplete suggestions will appear as you type — tap one to select it
Set Your Dropoff Location
  • Country: Select the destination country (can be different from pickup)
  • City: Type and select your destination city from the suggestions
Tip: The city database covers 150+ Canadian cities, 300+ US cities, 80+ Philippine cities, and 70+ Indian cities. City names include province or state codes for clarity (e.g., "Vancouver, BC" or "Toronto, ON").

Section 2: Trip Dates

Set Your Schedule
  • Departure Date: Select the day you'll be leaving (must be today or later)
  • Departure Time: Set your departure time (must be at least 5 minutes from now)
  • Arrival Date: Select the day you'll arrive (must be on or after the departure date)
  • Arrival Time: Set your expected arrival time (must be at least 20 minutes after departure)
Date Rules: Your trip must be at least 20 minutes long. Departure defaults to tomorrow and arrival to the day after — adjust as needed for your travel plans.

Section 3: Available Space

Set Your Weight Capacity
  • Weight Capacity (kg): Enter how much weight you can carry, from 0.01 to 1,000 kg

This tells shippers the maximum package weight you can accommodate on your trip.

Section 4: Transportation Method

Choose How You're Traveling

Select your mode of transportation:

  • Car
  • Bus
  • Truck
  • Van
  • Motorcycle
  • Train
  • Ship
  • Flight

The default is Car. Your transportation method determines how pricing works in the next section.

Section 5: Your Price

Pricing works differently depending on your transportation method:

For Flights — Per-Kilogram Pricing

  • Set a price per kilogram (e.g., $5.00/kg)
  • Range: $0.01 to $1,000 per kg
  • The total cost for a shipper is calculated as: package weight × your price per kg

Example: If you set $5.00/kg and a shipper has a 3 kg package, their delivery cost would be $15.00.

For Land Transport — Flat Trip Pricing

Car, Bus, Truck, Van, Motorcycle, Train, and Ship all use flat pricing:

  • Set a flat trip price for your entire route (e.g., $150.00)
  • Minimum: $5.00 CAD
  • Maximum: $10,000
  • This is the total price regardless of package weight

Example: If you set $150.00 flat for a Vancouver → Calgary route, that's the delivery cost for any package within your weight capacity.

Pricing Tip: Consider the distance, fuel costs, and effort when setting your price. Competitive pricing helps attract more shippers while ensuring you're fairly compensated. Remember, a 5% service fee will be deducted from your earnings.

Section 6: Notes (Optional)

Add Special Notes

Optionally add any notes for potential shippers. This is a good place to mention:

  • Types of packages you prefer or can't carry
  • Special handling capabilities (e.g., "Can transport fragile items carefully")
  • Flexibility on timing or route
  • Any restrictions (e.g., "No liquids or perishables")

Submitting Your Trip

Review and Create

Once all 4 requirements in the progress banner are met (shown in green), tap "Create Trip" to publish your trip.

You'll see a success confirmation, and your trip will immediately become visible to shippers with compatible packages.

Validation: If any required fields are missing, the "Create Trip" button will be disabled. The progress banner shows exactly which requirements still need to be completed.

Trip Statuses

After creation, your trip progresses through these statuses:

  • Planning: Trip is published and visible to shippers — waiting for package bookings
  • Active: One or more packages have been confirmed on your trip
  • In Transit: You've started traveling and are delivering packages
  • Completed: Trip finished and all packages delivered
  • Cancelled: Trip was cancelled

After Creating Your Trip

Getting Matched with Packages

Once your trip is published:

  • Shippers browse compatible trips: Shippers with packages along your route can see your trip and request a match
  • Review requests: You'll receive notifications when a shipper is interested
  • Accept or decline: Review the package details, shipper profile, and pricing before deciding

Managing Your Trip

  • View your trips from the Dashboard or Carrier section
  • Track matched packages and their statuses
  • Communicate with shippers through in-app chat
Tip: Create trips for your regular travel routes — even daily commutes! Shippers in your area may have packages that fit perfectly along your existing route.

Supported Countries

Pasabayan currently supports trips in:

  • Canada — 150+ cities across all provinces and territories
  • United States — 300+ cities covering major metros and regional hubs
  • Philippines — 80+ cities including Manila, Cebu, and Davao
  • India — 70+ cities including Delhi, Mumbai, and Bangalore

You can create trips between different countries for international deliveries.

Need Help?

If you encounter any issues creating a trip:

  • Make sure you're registered as a carrier (switch roles from your Profile if needed)
  • Check that all 4 requirements are completed in the progress banner
  • Submit a support ticket through the app if you need assistance
