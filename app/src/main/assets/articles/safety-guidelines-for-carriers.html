Safety Guidelines for Carriers

Your safety is our priority. Follow these guidelines to ensure safe and successful deliveries while protecting yourself and your packages.

Before You Accept a Request

Verify Request Details

  • Review package description carefully
  • Confirm pickup and delivery locations
  • Check package weight and dimensions
  • Ensure you have adequate space
  • Read any special instructions
Prohibited Items: Never transport illegal substances, weapons, hazardous materials, live animals, or stolen goods. Report suspicious requests immediately.

Vehicle Safety

Pre-Trip Checklist

  • Vehicle is in good working condition
  • Valid registration and insurance
  • Full tank of gas
  • GPS or navigation app ready
  • Phone fully charged
  • Emergency contact information updated

During Pickup

When Meeting the Shipper

  • Public Location: Always meet in a well-lit, public area
  • Verify Identity: Confirm you're meeting the correct person
  • Inspect Package: Check for damage before accepting
  • Confirm Contents: Verify items match the description
  • Take Photos: Document package condition
  • Secure Properly: Position package safely in vehicle
Tip: If anything seems suspicious or the package doesn't match the description, politely decline and contact support.

Securing Packages

In Your Vehicle

  • Use seatbelt or tie-down straps
  • Keep fragile items separate
  • Prevent shifting during turns
  • Don't block your view
  • Secure doors and windows

During Transit

Safe Driving Practices

  • Obey all traffic laws
  • No texting or distracted driving
  • Take breaks on long trips
  • Follow recommended route
  • Keep package in sight if stopping
  • Update shipper on progress

At Delivery

Meeting Recipient

  • Verify Recipient: Confirm identity when required
  • Get Signature: Use confirmation code
  • Take Photo: Document delivery completion
  • Confirm Location: Ensure correct address
  • Professional: Be courteous and timely

Personal Safety

Red Flags - Trust Your Instincts

  • Suspicious meeting locations
  • Unclear or conflicting requests
  • Pressure to accept low offers
  • Requests to deviate from policy
  • Uncomfortable interactions

If You Feel Unsafe

  • Trust your instincts
  • Leave the situation immediately
  • Report the incident to support
  • Contact emergency services if needed
  • Document what happened

Communication Safety

In-App Messaging Only

  • Use Pasabayan's messaging system
  • Don't share personal phone numbers
  • Keep all communication in-app
  • Report inappropriate messages
  • Contact support if issues arise

Insurance Coverage

What's Protected

  • Platform liability insurance for deliveries
  • Coverage up to declared package value
  • Protection against damage or loss
  • Dispute resolution process

Emergency Procedures

Accidents

  1. Ensure your safety first
  2. Call emergency services if needed
  3. Document the incident with photos
  4. Contact Pasabayan support immediately
  5. File official police report if required

Package Issues

  • Damage discovered: Contact support immediately
  • Lost package: Report through app
  • Wrong address: Contact shipper before delivering
  • Recipient unavailable: Follow app guidelines

Best Practices

Build Your Reputation

  • Be punctual for pickups and deliveries
  • Communicate clearly and regularly
  • Handle packages with care
  • Be professional and courteous
  • Ask for ratings after successful deliveries

Legal Compliance

  • Valid driver's license required
  • Vehicle must be legally registered
  • Follow all traffic laws
  • Respect delivery restrictions
  • Report illegal activities

Need Help?

Safety concerns or questions? Contact us:

  • Emergency: Call 911
  • Safety Hotline: Available 24/7 in-app
  • Support: Live Chat or email
  • Report Safety Issues: Through Help Center

Remember: Your safety comes first. When in doubt, contact support or law enforcement.
