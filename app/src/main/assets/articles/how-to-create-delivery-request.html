How to Create a Delivery Request

Pasabayan offers two ways to get things delivered: Ship a Package for sending parcels, or Errand Service for grocery shopping, food delivery, pharmacy pickups, and more. Follow the steps below for each option.

Getting Started

Step 1: Navigate to the Packages Tab

Open the Pasabayan app and tap on the "Packages" tab at the bottom of your screen. This is where you manage all your delivery requests.

Step 2: Tap the "CREATE" Button

Tap the floating "CREATE" button at the bottom-right corner of the screen. A sheet will appear with two options:

  • Ship Package — For sending parcels and items to another location
  • Errand Service — For grocery shopping, food delivery, pharmacy pickups, and general errands

Choose the option that fits your needs.

Option A: Ship a Package

Use this flow when you need to send a parcel or item from one location to another.

Step 3A: Add Photos (Optional)

Upload up to 5 photos of your package to help carriers know what to expect. Each image can be up to 5MB in JPG or PNG format.

Step 4A: Fill in Package Details

Provide the following information:

  • Package Description: Briefly describe what you're sending (required)
  • Weight: Enter the weight in kilograms (required)
  • Package Type: Select a category — Electronics, Fragile, Documents, Books, Clothing, Food, Furniture, Medical, Gifts, and more
  • Fragile: Toggle on if your package requires careful handling
  • Package Value: Declare the approximate value (optional)
  • Max Budget: The maximum you're willing to pay for delivery (optional)
  • Urgency Level: Select Low, Normal, High, Urgent, Express, or Flexible
  • Special Handling Requirements: Any specific instructions for the carrier (optional)
Step 5A: Set Pickup Information

Enter your pickup details:

  • Country: Select from the country picker
  • City: Type to search — suggestions appear as you type
  • Address: Enter the full pickup address
  • Postal Code: (optional)
  • Landmark: A nearby landmark to help the carrier find you (optional)
  • Preferred Pickup Date and Time: Choose when you'd like the package picked up
  • Flexible Pickup: Toggle on if your pickup date is flexible
Step 6A: Set Delivery Information

Enter the delivery destination with the same fields: country, city, address, postal code, landmark, and preferred delivery date and time.

Step 7A: Review and Submit

A requirements counter at the top shows your progress (e.g., "3/5"). All 5 requirements must be met before you can submit:

  • Package description filled in
  • Weight entered
  • Pickup address and city provided
  • Delivery address and city provided
  • Valid dates selected

Once complete, tap "Create Request" to submit. You'll see a confirmation, and the form will clear automatically.

Duplicate Check: If a similar package request already exists, the app will warn you before creating a duplicate. You can choose to proceed or cancel.

Option B: Errand Service

Use this flow when you need someone to shop, pick up, or run an errand for you.

Step 3B: Select Service Type

Choose the type of errand you need:

  • Grocery Shopping — Someone shops for your grocery items
  • Food Delivery — Pick up a food order for you
  • Pharmacy Pickup — Collect prescriptions or pharmacy items
  • General Errand — Any other task you need help with
Step 4B: Create Your Shopping List

Add the items you need. The list title changes based on service type (e.g., "Items List" for groceries, "Order List" for food, "Prescription List" for pharmacy).

For each item, provide:

  • Item Name: What you need (required)
  • Quantity: How many (required)
  • Notes: Brand preferences, size, or other details (optional)

Tap "Add Item" to add more items. You can remove items with the delete button. Suggestions may appear as you type to help you add items faster.

Step 5B: Set Delivery Location

Tell the carrier where to deliver:

  • Delivery Address: Enter your address (autocomplete suggestions appear as you type)
  • Delivery City: Type to search from available cities
  • Interactive Map: You can also tap the map to select your location, or use the "Use My Location" button for quick entry
Step 6B: Optional Settings

Expand the "Optional Details" section to add:

  • Preferred Store: Store name and address if you have a preference (carrier can choose if left blank)
  • Estimated Total: Your estimate of what the items will cost
  • Max Budget: The maximum you're willing to spend overall
  • Delivery Date: When you need the items delivered (minimum is tomorrow)
  • Special Instructions: Any additional notes for the carrier
Step 7B: Submit Your Request

A requirements banner at the top tracks your progress (2 steps required):

  • Shopping list has items with names and quantities
  • Delivery address and city provided

Once both are complete, tap "Send Request" to submit.

After Submission

Matching with Carriers

After your request is created, it becomes visible to carriers traveling compatible routes. You can:

  • Browse Compatible Trips: See carriers heading in your direction and request a match
  • Accept or Decline Offers: When a carrier is interested, you can review their profile and decide

Tracking Your Request

Once your request is matched with a carrier, you can track its progress:

  • Status Updates: Track from "Open" → "Matched" → "Picked Up" → "In Transit" → "Delivered"
  • Notifications: Receive updates when your package status changes
  • Chat: Message your carrier directly through the in-app chat
Tip: Add clear special instructions for your carrier. This could include gate codes, delivery preferences, or handling notes for fragile items.
