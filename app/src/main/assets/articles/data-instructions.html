Manage Your Data

Last updated: February 2026

1. Your Data at Pasabayan
▼

Pasabayan collects and stores data to provide you with a safe and seamless delivery experience. You have full control over your personal data and can manage it at any time through the app or by contacting our support team.

This page explains what data we store, how to access it, and how to request changes or deletion.

2. What Data We Store
▼

Account Information:

  • Name, email address, and phone number
  • Profile photo and bio
  • Account verification status
  • Linked social login accounts (Google, Facebook, Apple)

Activity Data:

  • Delivery requests and trip history
  • Booking records and transaction history
  • Ratings and reviews given or received
  • Messages exchanged with other users

Payment Data:

  • Payment method details (processed securely by Stripe)
  • Payout information for carriers
  • Transaction receipts

Device & Location Data:

  • Device identifiers and push notification tokens
  • Location data (only when the app is in use)
3. How to Access Your Data
▼

You can view most of your data directly in the app:

  • Profile: Go to Settings > Profile to see your personal information
  • Bookings: View your delivery and trip history in the Bookings tab
  • Payments: Check transaction history in Settings > Payments
  • Ratings: See your ratings and reviews on your profile

To request a full copy of all data we hold about you, contact us at support@pasabayan.com with the subject line "Data Access Request." We will provide your data in a portable format within 30 days.

4. How to Update Your Information
▼

You can update your personal information at any time:

  1. Open the Pasabayan app
  2. Go to Settings > Profile
  3. Tap on the field you want to edit (name, email, phone, photo)
  4. Make your changes and save

Note: Changing your email or phone number may require re-verification for security purposes.

If you need to update information that cannot be changed through the app (such as correcting transaction records), please contact support@pasabayan.com.

5. How to Download Your Data
▼

You have the right to receive a copy of your personal data in a structured, commonly used format. To request a data export:

  1. Send an email to support@pasabayan.com
  2. Use the subject line: "Data Export Request"
  3. Include your registered email address and full name
  4. We may ask you to verify your identity for security

Your data export will be delivered within 30 days and will include your profile information, activity history, and transaction records.

6. How to Delete Your Data
▼

You can request deletion of your account and personal data:

  1. Open the Pasabayan app
  2. Go to Settings > Account > Delete Account
  3. Confirm your identity
  4. Review what will be deleted and confirm

Alternatively, email support@pasabayan.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request."

What happens when you delete your account:

  • Your profile and personal information will be permanently removed
  • Your delivery and trip history will be anonymized
  • Your ratings and reviews will be disassociated from your identity
  • Active bookings must be completed or cancelled before deletion
  • Pending payouts will be processed before account closure

Note: Some data may be retained for up to 90 days for legal and security purposes, after which it will be permanently deleted.

7. Managing Permissions
▼

You can control what data Pasabayan can access through your device settings:

Location:

  • Go to your device's Settings > Privacy > Location Services > Pasabayan
  • Choose "While Using the App," "Ask Next Time," or "Never"
  • Disabling location may limit trip matching and tracking features

Notifications:

  • Go to your device's Settings > Notifications > Pasabayan
  • Toggle notifications on or off
  • Disabling notifications means you may miss booking updates and messages

Camera & Photos:

  • Go to your device's Settings > Privacy > Camera / Photos > Pasabayan
  • Used for profile photos and package documentation
8. Managing Connected Accounts
▼

If you signed up using a social login (Google, Facebook, or Apple), you can manage these connections:

  • Go to Settings > Account > Connected Accounts
  • View which social accounts are linked to your Pasabayan profile
  • Disconnect a social account (you must have an email/password set up first)

Disconnecting a social account does not delete your Pasabayan account. It only removes the link between the two services.

9. Payment Data & Stripe
▼

Pasabayan uses Stripe to securely process payments. Here's what you should know:

  • Your full card details are never stored on Pasabayan's servers
  • Payment information is managed directly by Stripe
  • You can manage saved payment methods in Settings > Payments
  • Carrier payout details are handled through Stripe Connect

To remove your payment data from Stripe, you can delete your saved payment methods in the app or request full removal by contacting support@pasabayan.com.

10. Data Breach Notification
▼

In the unlikely event of a data breach that affects your personal information, we will:

  • Notify affected users within 72 hours of discovery
  • Describe the nature of the breach and data affected
  • Explain what measures we are taking to address it
  • Provide recommendations for protecting yourself
  • Report to relevant data protection authorities as required by law

Notifications will be sent via email and in-app alerts.

11. Contact & Support
▼

For any questions about your data or to make a request, contact us:

Email: support@pasabayan.com

In-App: Settings > Support > Contact Us

We aim to respond to all data-related requests within 30 days. For urgent matters, please indicate "Urgent" in your subject line.
