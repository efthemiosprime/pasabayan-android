Service Agreement

Last updated: January 2026

ℹ️ This agreement outlines the terms for using Pasabayan's delivery services, including payment terms, cancellation policies, and user responsibilities.

1. Service Overview
▼

Pasabayan provides a peer-to-peer delivery platform connecting:

  • Shippers: Individuals who need to send packages
  • Carriers: Individuals traveling who can transport packages

By using our services, both shippers and carriers agree to the terms outlined in this Service Agreement.

This agreement, together with the Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, and Liability Waiver, forms the complete agreement between you and Pasabayan.

2. Account Requirements
▼

All Users Must:

  • Be at least 18 years of age
  • Provide accurate and complete registration information
  • Verify their phone number
  • Maintain a valid payment method
  • Keep account information up to date

Additional Requirements for Carriers:

  • Hold a valid driver's license
  • Maintain valid vehicle insurance
  • Complete identity verification
  • Set up Stripe Connect for payments
  • Have access to a vehicle in good working condition
3. Shipper Responsibilities
▼

As a shipper, you agree to:

  • Accurate Information: Provide truthful package descriptions, dimensions, weight, and contents
  • Proper Packaging: Ensure items are securely packaged to prevent damage during transport
  • Prohibited Items: Not request transport of prohibited, illegal, or dangerous items
  • Availability: Be available at the agreed pickup time and location
  • Communication: Respond promptly to carrier messages
  • Payment: Pay the agreed delivery fee before pickup
  • Confirmation: Confirm delivery receipt in the app
4. Carrier Responsibilities
▼

As a carrier, you agree to:

  • Trip Accuracy: Post accurate trip details including route, dates, and available capacity
  • Package Care: Handle all packages with reasonable care during transport
  • Timely Delivery: Deliver packages within the agreed timeframe
  • Communication: Keep shippers informed of any delays or issues
  • Verification: Verify package contents match the description at pickup
  • Legal Compliance: Follow all traffic laws and transportation regulations
  • Insurance: Maintain valid auto insurance at all times
  • Prohibited Items: Refuse to transport prohibited or suspicious items
5. Payment Terms
▼

For Shippers:

  • Payment is required to confirm a delivery booking
  • Shippers pay the agreed delivery price plus a 10% service fee
  • All prices are in Canadian Dollars (CAD)
  • Payment is processed securely through Stripe
  • Funds are held in escrow until delivery is confirmed

For Carriers:

  • Payment is released after shipper confirms delivery
  • Carriers receive the agreed delivery price minus a 5% service fee
  • Payouts are processed via Stripe Connect to your linked bank account
  • Payout timing depends on your Stripe payout schedule
  • You are responsible for reporting and paying applicable taxes

Service Fees:

  • Shipper service fee: 10% of the agreed delivery price (added to shipper's total)
  • Carrier service fee: 5% of the agreed delivery price (deducted from carrier payout)
  • All fees are clearly displayed before either party confirms a transaction
6. Cancellation Policy
▼

Shipper Cancellations:

  • 24+ hours before pickup: Full refund
  • 12-24 hours before pickup: 75% refund
  • Less than 12 hours: 50% refund
  • No-show at pickup: No refund

Carrier Cancellations:

  • 24+ hours before pickup: No penalty
  • Less than 24 hours: May affect rating and account standing
  • Repeated cancellations: May result in account restrictions

Mutual Cancellations: If both parties agree to cancel, a full refund will be issued to the shipper.

Force Majeure: In cases of severe weather, accidents, or emergencies, cancellations may be handled on a case-by-case basis.

7. Refund Policy
▼

Refunds may be issued in the following circumstances:

  • Non-delivery: Full refund if carrier fails to complete delivery
  • Significant delay: Partial refund may be considered
  • Damaged items: Claim reviewed on case-by-case basis
  • Service not as described: Partial or full refund after investigation

Refund Process:

  • Submit a refund request through the app within 48 hours
  • Provide supporting evidence (photos, messages)
  • Pasabayan will review and respond within 5 business days
  • Approved refunds are processed within 5-10 business days

Refunds are issued to the original payment method.

8. Ratings and Reviews
▼

Both shippers and carriers can rate each other after completed deliveries.

Rating Guidelines:

  • Ratings should be honest and fair
  • Ratings cannot be exchanged for benefits
  • Retaliatory ratings are prohibited
  • Ratings can only be submitted within 7 days of delivery

Impact of Ratings:

  • Average rating below 4.0: Account review
  • Average rating below 3.5: Possible account restrictions
  • Consistent low ratings: May result in account suspension

Pasabayan may remove ratings that violate our guidelines.

10. Account Standing
▼

You may appeal account actions by contacting support@pasabayan.com.
