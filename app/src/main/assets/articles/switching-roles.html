Switching Between Sender and Carrier

Last updated: November 2025

Understanding Dual Roles
▼

Pasabayan allows you to act as both a Sender (sending packages) and a Carrier (transporting packages). You can switch between these roles at any time to access different features.

Sender Role: Create package requests, browse available trips, request carriers, and track your deliveries.

Carrier Role: Create trips, manage capacity, accept package requests, and earn money by delivering packages.

💡 Tip: Most users start as senders and later enable carrier mode to earn extra income during their regular travels.
How to Switch Roles
▼

Follow these simple steps to switch between sender and carrier roles:

  1. 1 Locate the User Header
    At the top of your Home screen, you'll see a card with your profile information, including your name and avatar.
  2. 2 Tap the Three-Dot Menu
    In the bottom right corner of the User Header card, tap the three-dot menu (⋮) icon.
  3. 3 Select Your Desired Role
    A menu will appear with options:
    • Switch to Sender - Access sender features
    • Switch to Carrier - Access carrier features
  4. 4 Wait for Activation
    If switching to carrier for the first time, the app will enable carrier features for your account. This only takes a moment.
  5. 5 Start Using the New Role
    Once switched, you'll see the dashboard and features specific to your selected role.
Identifying Your Current Role
▼

You can always see which role you're currently using by checking the User Header card at the top of your Home screen:

  • Role Badge: A small badge displays either "Sender" or "Carrier"
  • Dashboard Features: The available tabs and features reflect your current role
  • Verification Badge: Next to the role badge, you'll see your verification level (Basic, Verified, or Premium)
💡 Tip: The role badge and verification level are displayed side by side in the User Header card for easy reference.
Enabling Carrier Mode for the First Time
▼

If you're switching to carrier mode for the first time, here's what happens:

  • Automatic Activation: The app automatically enables carrier features for your account
  • Profile Setup: You'll be able to set up your carrier profile, including vehicle information and preferred routes
  • Capacity Management: You can define how much space and weight you can carry
  • Pricing Control: Set your own rates per kilogram or per delivery

Requirements for Carrier Mode:

  • Valid phone number (must be verified)
  • Complete profile information
  • Agreement to carrier terms and safety guidelines
Features by Role
▼

Sender Features:

  • Create and manage package requests
  • Browse available carrier trips
  • Filter carriers by route, price, and ratings
  • Request specific carriers for your packages
  • Track packages in real-time
  • Rate carriers after delivery
  • View delivery history

Carrier Features:

  • Create and manage trips
  • Set available capacity (weight and space)
  • Browse available package requests
  • Accept or invite senders to your trips
  • Manage multiple deliveries in one trip
  • Update trip status and location
  • View earnings and analytics
  • Rate senders after delivery
Switching During Active Deliveries
▼

You can switch roles even if you have active deliveries:

  • In Progress: Active deliveries remain accessible regardless of your current role
  • Notifications: You'll receive notifications for all active deliveries in both roles
  • Messages: The Messages tab shows conversations from both sender and carrier activities
  • Match Details: You can view and manage all active matches from either role
💡 Tip: If you have an urgent carrier task while in sender mode, just switch roles temporarily and switch back when done.
Role Preferences
▼

Pasabayan remembers your role preference:

  • Last Used Role: When you open the app, it starts with the last role you were using
  • Quick Switch: The three-dot menu makes it easy to switch roles in just two taps
  • Persistent Settings: Your role preference is saved locally on your device

To set a default role preference:

  1. Go to Profile → Settings
  2. Under Role Preference, select your preferred default role
  3. The app will remember this setting across sessions
Frequently Asked Questions
▼

Q: Can I be a sender and carrier at the same time?

A: You can only view one role at a time in the app, but you can have active deliveries in both roles simultaneously. Simply switch between roles as needed.

Q: Is there a limit to how many times I can switch roles?

A: No, you can switch between roles as often as you need. There are no restrictions or cooldown periods.

Q: Do I need to verify my account separately for each role?

A: No, your account verification (phone verification, identity verification) applies to both roles. However, carriers may have additional requirements like vehicle information.

Q: Will my ratings be different for sender and carrier roles?

A: Yes, you have separate ratings for each role. Your sender rating is based on how you perform as a sender, and your carrier rating reflects your performance as a carrier.

Q: What happens if I disable carrier mode?

A: If you complete all your carrier deliveries and decide to stop being a carrier, you can disable carrier mode in your profile settings. Your carrier history and ratings will be preserved.
