Payment Methods and Billing

Pasabayan uses Stripe for secure payment processing to ensure your transactions are safe and protected. Learn about payment methods, billing processes, and how to manage your account finances.

Accepted Payment Methods

Credit and Debit Cards

We accept all major credit and debit cards including:

  • Visa
  • Mastercard
  • American Express
  • Discover

Apple Pay is also supported on compatible devices.

Secure Processing: All card information is processed and stored securely through Stripe, a PCI DSS Level 1 certified payment processor. We never store your full card number on our servers.

Adding a Payment Method

  1. Go to Profile: Tap on the "Profile" tab at the bottom of your screen
  2. Navigate to Payments: Tap the "Payment Methods" option in the Payments section
  3. Add Card: Tap the "+ Add Payment Method" button
  4. Complete the Secure Form: A secure Stripe payment form will appear where you can enter your card details or use Apple Pay
  5. Done: Once completed, your card is securely saved to your account for future payments
Tip: You can add multiple cards and set one as your default payment method for faster checkout. Swipe on a card to remove it or tap to set it as default.

How Billing Works

For Shippers (Package Senders)

When You're Charged

  • Match Confirmed: When both you and a carrier agree on the delivery, you'll be prompted to complete payment
  • Payment Authorization: When you complete the payment form, the agreed amount is authorized (held) on your card but not yet captured
  • Payment Captured: The charge is captured and finalized once the payment is confirmed
Important: You must initiate payment after your match is confirmed. Payment is not automatic — you'll see a "Pay Now" option to complete the transaction using your saved card or a new payment method.

For Carriers (Drivers)

Earnings and Payouts

  • Commission Structure: You receive the agreed delivery fee minus a 5% carrier service fee
  • Stripe Connect: Payouts are processed through your Stripe Connect account, which must be set up before you can receive payments
  • Payout Processing: Once a transaction is completed, funds are transferred to your linked bank account through Stripe

Setting Up Payouts (Carriers)

To receive earnings as a carrier, you need to complete Stripe Connect onboarding:

  1. Go to Profile: Tap on the "Profile" tab
  2. Payout Setup: Tap the "Payout Setup" option in the Payments section
  3. Complete Onboarding: You'll be directed to Stripe's secure onboarding page where you'll provide:
    • Identity verification (KYC)
    • Bank account details for receiving payouts
    • Tax information
  4. Return to App: Once onboarding is complete, your payout status will be verified automatically
Tip: After onboarding, you can access your Stripe Express Dashboard from the Payout Setup screen to view your balance, payout history, and bank account details.

Service Fees

Fee Structure

  • Shipper Service Fee: 10% of the agreed delivery price is added to your total as a platform fee
  • Carrier Service Fee: 5% of the agreed delivery price is deducted from your earnings
  • All fees are clearly displayed before you confirm any transaction

Example Breakdown

For an agreed delivery price of $100 CAD:

  • Shipper pays: $100 + $10 service fee = $110 total
  • Carrier receives: $100 - $5 service fee = $95 earnings
Minimum Delivery Price: The minimum agreed delivery price is $5.00 CAD.

Transaction History

View all your payment activity through the Transaction History:

  1. Go to Profile → Payments section → Transaction History
  2. Filter transactions by role: All, Shipper, or Carrier
  3. Tap on any transaction to view full details including fee breakdowns and payout status

What You'll See

  • Transaction date and time
  • Delivery reference and other party details
  • Amount charged or earned
  • Service fee breakdown
  • Payment and payout status

Receipts

Download PDF receipts for your records:

  1. Go to Profile → Payments section → Receipts
  2. Browse your receipt history
  3. Tap on a receipt to view the full PDF document
Tip: Receipts show different amounts depending on your role — shippers see what they paid, and carriers see what they earned. Keep receipts for tax purposes or expense tracking.

Cancellations and Refunds

Cancellation

You can cancel a payment from the transaction detail screen. Cancelled payments will be reversed if the charge was authorized or captured.

Requesting a Refund

  1. Go to the transaction detail for the delivery in question
  2. Tap "Request Refund"
  3. Select a reason for the refund:
    • Package was damaged during delivery
    • Package was never delivered
    • Wrong item was delivered
    • Delivery was significantly late
    • Only partial items were delivered
    • Other (provide details)
  4. Optionally request a partial refund by entering a specific amount
  5. Add any additional details to support your request
  6. Submit the refund request for review

Refund Review Process

  • Your refund request is reviewed by our support team
  • You can check the status of your request from the transaction detail
  • Once approved, the refund is processed through Stripe back to your original payment method
Important: Refund eligibility depends on specific circumstances and is evaluated on a case-by-case basis. Please provide as much detail as possible when submitting your request.

Payment Security

We Protect Your Financial Information

  • PCI DSS Compliant: Meets highest industry security standards through Stripe
  • Tokenization: Card numbers are encrypted and tokenized — we never see your full card number
  • 3D Secure: Additional verification is automatically triggered when required by your bank
  • Secure Storage: Authentication credentials stored in iOS Keychain
  • Stripe Connect: Carrier payouts processed through Stripe's verified platform

Common Billing Questions

Why was I charged twice?

If you see two transactions, one may be an authorization (hold) and one the actual charge. Authorizations are temporary and will be released by your bank.

Can I change my payment method for an active delivery?

You can choose your payment method when completing the payment form. Once a payment has been authorized, the selected payment method is used.

How do I set up payouts as a carrier?

Go to Profile → Payments section → Payout Setup. You'll complete Stripe Connect onboarding, which includes identity verification and linking your bank account.

How much are the service fees?

Shippers pay a 10% service fee on top of the agreed delivery price. Carriers have a 5% service fee deducted from their earnings. All fees are shown before you confirm any transaction.

Are there any hidden fees?

No hidden fees! All charges including service fees are clearly displayed before you confirm any transaction.
