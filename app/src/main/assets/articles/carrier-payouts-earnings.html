Carrier Payouts and Earnings

As a carrier on Pasabayan, you earn money by delivering packages for shippers along your travel routes. This guide explains how to set up payouts, understand your earnings, and track your income.

Setting Up Payouts (Stripe Connect)

Before you can receive earnings, you need to complete Stripe Connect onboarding. This is a one-time setup that links your bank account for automatic payouts.

Step 1: Navigate to Payout Setup

Open the Pasabayan app and go to Profile tab → Payments section → Payout Setup.

Step 2: Start Onboarding

Tap the "Setup" button. You'll be directed to Stripe's secure onboarding page in your browser. If you've started before but didn't finish, you'll see a "Continue Setup" button instead.

Step 3: Complete Stripe Verification

Stripe will ask you to provide:

  • Identity Verification: Government-issued ID for KYC (Know Your Customer) compliance
  • Bank Account Details: Your bank account where payouts will be deposited
  • Address Verification: Confirm your current address
  • Tax Information: Required tax details for your region
Step 4: Return to the App

Once onboarding is complete, return to the Pasabayan app. Your payout status will be verified automatically. You'll see your status change to "Active" with a "View Dashboard" button.

Secure Processing: All financial information is processed through Stripe, a PCI DSS Level 1 certified payment processor. Pasabayan never stores your bank account details directly.

Payout Setup Statuses

  • Not Onboarded: You haven't started setup yet — tap "Setup" to begin
  • Incomplete: You started onboarding but didn't finish — tap "Continue Setup" to resume
  • Active: Fully set up and ready to receive payouts — tap "View Dashboard" to manage your account

How You Get Paid

Payment Flow

  1. Delivery Matched: A shipper's package is matched with your trip and both parties confirm
  2. Shipper Pays: The shipper completes payment, which is authorized (held) by Stripe
  3. You Deliver: Pick up the package using the 6-digit pickup code and deliver it to the destination
  4. Delivery Confirmed: The shipper provides you with the delivery confirmation code to complete the handoff
  5. Payment Captured: Once delivery is confirmed, the payment is captured and your earnings are processed
  6. Payout Transferred: Stripe automatically transfers your earnings to your connected bank account
Important: You must complete Stripe Connect onboarding before you can receive any payouts. Set this up before accepting your first delivery.

Understanding Your Earnings

Fee Structure

For each delivery, here's how the money flows:

  • Agreed Delivery Price: The base price agreed upon between you and the shipper
  • Carrier Service Fee: 5% of the agreed price is deducted as a platform fee
  • You Receive: The agreed price minus the 5% service fee
  • Tips: Shippers can add a tip after delivery — tips go directly to you with no deduction

Example Breakdown

For an agreed delivery price of $100 CAD:

  • Agreed Price: $100.00
  • Service Fee (5%): -$5.00
  • You Receive: $95.00
  • If Shipper Tips $10: $95.00 + $10.00 = $105.00 total
Tip: All fees are transparently displayed before you confirm any delivery. There are no hidden charges.

Viewing Your Earnings

Transaction History

Track all your earnings through your transaction history:

  1. Go to Profile → Payments section → Transaction History
  2. Filter by role: tap "Carrier" to see only your earnings
  3. Tap any transaction to view full details

Transaction Details Include

  • Earnings Summary: Delivery earnings, service fee deduction, and final amount received
  • Payout Status: Whether the payout has been processed, is pending, or completed
  • Timeline: Status progression from created to completed
  • Delivery Info: Package details, route, and shipper information
  • Tip Amount: Any gratuity added by the shipper

Stripe Express Dashboard

For a complete view of your finances, access your Stripe Express Dashboard:

  1. Go to Profile → Payments section → Payout Setup
  2. Tap "View Dashboard"
  3. View your balance, payout history, and bank account details directly on Stripe

Payout Statuses

Each payout goes through these stages:

  • Pending: Payout is scheduled but not yet processed
  • Processing: Payout is being transferred to your bank
  • Completed: Funds have been deposited to your bank account
  • On Hold: Payout is temporarily held — contact support if this persists
  • Failed: Payout could not be processed — check your bank details in the Stripe Dashboard
Payout Timing: After delivery confirmation, payouts are typically processed within 2–5 business days, depending on your bank and region. You can check the exact timing in your Stripe Express Dashboard.

Receipts

Download receipts for your records and tax purposes:

  1. Go to Profile → Payments section → Receipts
  2. Browse your receipt history
  3. Tap a receipt to view the full PDF document

Receipt Details

  • Receipt number and date
  • Delivery route (pickup → delivery city)
  • Carrier earnings amount (including tips)
  • Service fee breakdown
  • Shipper information
Tip: Keep your receipts for tax reporting. Your carrier receipts show exactly what you earned for each delivery.

Tips from Shippers

Shippers can add a tip after a delivery is completed as a thank-you for great service. Tips are:

  • 100% yours — no platform fee is deducted from tips
  • Processed separately through Stripe
  • Included in your transaction details and receipt totals

Common Questions

When can I start receiving payouts?

As soon as your Stripe Connect onboarding is complete and your status shows "Active." Make sure to set this up before accepting deliveries.

Why is my payout on hold?

Payouts may be held if Stripe requires additional verification or if there's an issue with the transaction. Check your Stripe Express Dashboard for details, or contact support.

Can I change my bank account?

Yes. Access your Stripe Express Dashboard from Profile → Payout Setup → View Dashboard to update your bank account details.

How do I see my total earnings?

Use Transaction History filtered to "Carrier" for in-app earnings, or check your Stripe Express Dashboard for a complete financial overview including payout history.

What's the minimum delivery price?

The minimum agreed delivery price is $5.00 CAD. This ensures fair compensation for carriers.

Need Help?

If you have questions about your payouts or earnings:

  • Check your Transaction History for delivery-specific details
  • Access your Stripe Express Dashboard for payout and bank information
  • Submit a support ticket through the app for billing inquiries
