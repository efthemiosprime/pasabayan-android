6. Matching and Price Negotiation
▼

Pasabayan facilitates matching between shippers and carriers. Either party may initiate a match request with a proposed price.

  • Shippers may browse carrier trips and submit requests with an offered price
  • Carriers may browse package requests and submit offers with a proposed price
  • Either party may accept, decline, or submit a counter-offer during negotiation
  • A limited number of counter-offer rounds are permitted per match
  • A match is confirmed when both parties agree on terms

Match requests that go unanswered may be automatically cancelled by the system after a specified period. Both parties will be notified of any auto-cancellation.

Once a match is confirmed, a private chat conversation is created for coordination. Cancellation is permitted before package pickup; after pickup, the delivery must be completed.

7. Delivery Confirmation and Security
▼

Pasabayan uses a secure confirmation code system to verify package handoffs at both pickup and delivery.

  • Pickup: The shipper generates a 6-digit pickup code. The carrier must enter this code to confirm they have collected the package.
  • Delivery: The shipper or recipient generates a 6-digit delivery code. The carrier must enter this code to confirm successful delivery.
  • Confirmation codes have a limited validity period and expire automatically
  • Codes are shared through the in-app chat for convenience

You must not share confirmation codes with anyone other than the matched shipper or carrier. Misuse of the confirmation code system may result in account suspension.

8. Payment and Fees
▼

Pasabayan facilitates payment between shippers and carriers through Stripe, a PCI DSS Level 1 certified payment processor. Pasabayan does not store your full card number or bank account details.

Fee Structure:

  • Shippers pay the agreed delivery price plus a 10% service fee
  • Carriers receive the agreed delivery price minus a 5% service fee
  • All fees are clearly displayed before either party confirms a transaction
  • The minimum agreed delivery price is $5.00 CAD
  • All prices are in Canadian Dollars (CAD)

Payment Flow:

  • Payment is authorized when a match is confirmed
  • Payment must be completed before the shipper can generate a pickup code
  • Payment is captured upon delivery confirmation
  • Carrier payouts are processed through Stripe Connect to the carrier's linked bank account

Tips: Shippers may add an optional tip after delivery completion. Tips are paid in full to the carrier with no platform deduction.

Refunds: Refund requests may be submitted through the app with a stated reason. Refunds are reviewed on a case-by-case basis. If a match is cancelled before pickup, applicable refunds are processed automatically.
