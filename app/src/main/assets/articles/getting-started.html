Getting Started with Pasabayan

Welcome to Pasabayan — the peer-to-peer delivery platform that connects shippers (people who need items delivered) with carriers (travelers who can carry items along their route). This guide walks you through everything you need to know to get up and running.

Creating Your Account

Step 1: Explore the Onboarding

When you first open Pasabayan, you'll see a guided onboarding flow that introduces the app. Start by choosing a role to explore:

  • "I'm a Carrier" — Earn money while you travel by delivering packages along your route
  • "I'm a Sender" — Send packages worldwide with trusted travelers at affordable prices

Don't worry — this is just to show you how each role works. You can use both roles at any time.

Step 2: Sign Up

After the onboarding, sign up using one of the following methods:

  • Apple Sign-In — Quick and private, using your Apple ID
  • Google Sign-In — Sign up with your Google account
  • Facebook Login — Sign up with your Facebook account

Your name, email, and profile photo are imported automatically from your chosen provider. No separate registration form needed.

Step 3: Accept Legal Agreements

On your first login, you'll be asked to review and accept the following documents:

  • Terms of Service
  • Privacy Policy
  • Service Agreement
  • Liability Waiver

Read each document and tap "I Agree" to proceed. You must accept all agreements before using the app.

Your Data is Secure: Authentication credentials are stored securely in iOS Keychain. All payments are processed through Stripe, a PCI DSS Level 1 certified payment processor. We never store your full card number or bank details.

Understanding Roles

Pasabayan users can hold both roles simultaneously. You can send packages as a shipper and earn money as a carrier — switch between roles anytime from your Profile.

Shipper (Sender)

You need something delivered. Create a package request with your item details, pickup and delivery locations, and budget. Browse carriers traveling compatible routes and request a match.

Carrier

You're traveling and have extra space. Post a trip with your route, dates, and available capacity. Shippers with packages along your route can request a match, or you can browse packages and offer to carry them.

Tip: To switch roles, go to your Profile tab and use the role switcher dropdown at the top. Your dashboard, tabs, and available features will adjust to match your current role.

Your Dashboard

After signing in, you'll land on your dashboard. The app is organized into 5 tabs at the bottom of the screen, which change based on your active role:

As a Shipper

  • Explore: Browse available carriers and trips heading your direction
  • Matches: Track your active deliveries and booking requests
  • My Packages: Manage your package requests — create new ones or view history
  • Messages: Chat with your matched carriers
  • Profile: Account settings, payments, verification, and more

As a Carrier

  • Explore: Browse available packages along your routes
  • Matches: View active bookings and delivery requests to accept or decline
  • My Trips: Manage your trips — create new ones or view history
  • Messages: Chat with your matched shippers
  • Profile: Account settings, payouts, vehicle info, and more

Getting Started as a Shipper

1. Create a Package Request

Go to the Packages tab and tap the "Create" button. Choose between:

  • Ship Package: Send a parcel from one location to another
  • Errand Service: Request grocery shopping, food delivery, pharmacy pickups, or general errands

Fill in your package details, pickup and delivery locations, dates, and budget.

2. Find a Carrier

Browse the Explore tab to see carriers traveling compatible routes. You can also wait for carriers to find your package request and send you an offer.

3. Match and Pay

When you find a carrier (or a carrier finds you), negotiate the price and confirm the match. Complete payment using your saved card or add a new payment method through Stripe's secure checkout.

4. Track Your Delivery

Once your package is picked up, track the carrier's location in real time from the Matches tab. Use in-app chat to stay in touch with your carrier throughout the delivery.

Tip: Add a payment method early by going to Profile → Payment Methods → Add Payment Method. This lets you complete transactions faster when you find a carrier.

Getting Started as a Carrier

1. Post a Trip

From your Dashboard, tap "Create Trip". Enter your route (pickup and destination cities), travel dates, available weight capacity, transportation method, and pricing. Once published, shippers along your route can find you.

2. Browse Packages

Check the Explore tab to see package requests from shippers along your route. You can send an offer with your proposed price.

3. Accept Requests

When a shipper requests a match with your trip, review the package details, price, and shipper profile. Accept, decline, or submit a counter-offer.

4. Deliver and Get Paid

Pick up the package using the secure confirmation code, deliver it to the destination, and confirm with the delivery code. Your earnings are automatically transferred to your bank account through Stripe.

Important: Set up your payout account before your first delivery. Go to Profile → Payout Setup to complete Stripe Connect onboarding. You'll need to provide identity verification and bank account details.

Verification Levels

Building trust is important on Pasabayan. Your account has a verification level that's visible to other users:

  • Basic: Your starting level after signing up with OAuth
  • Verified: Phone number verified — go to Profile → Phone Verification to verify with a 6-digit SMS code
  • Premium: Full identity verification with government ID — go to Profile → Premium Verification to apply
Tip: Higher verification levels build more trust with other users and can help you get matched faster. We recommend verifying your phone number as a first step.

Language Support

Pasabayan is available in English and French. You can switch languages from the onboarding welcome screen or from your device's language settings.

Supported Countries

Pasabayan currently supports routes in:

  • Canada — 150+ cities
  • United States — 300+ cities
  • Philippines — 80+ cities
  • India — 70+ cities

You can create trips and package requests between different countries for international deliveries.

Quick Tips for Success

  • Complete your profile: Add a profile photo and verify your phone number to build trust
  • Be responsive: Reply to match requests and messages promptly
  • Set fair prices: Competitive pricing helps you get matched faster
  • Communicate clearly: Use in-app chat to coordinate pickup details and share updates
  • Rate your experience: After each delivery, leave a rating and review — it helps the whole community
  • Explore both roles: You might travel a route where someone needs a delivery, or need something delivered along a route someone else is traveling
